Classic Shepherd's Pie
This recipe uses ground lamb, which is traditional for shepherd's pie. If you prefer beef, you'll be making cottage pie, but the method is the same!   

Yields: 6-8 servings
Prep time: 30 minutes
Cook time: 1 hour

Ingredients:

For the lamb filling:
    2 tablespoons olive oil
    1 large onion, chopped
    2 carrots, peeled and diced
    2 celery stalks, diced (optional, but adds great flavor)
    2 cloves garlic, minced   
    1.5 lbs ground lamb
    1 teaspoon dried rosemary   
    1 teaspoon dried thyme
    1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
    2 tablespoons tomato paste
    1 cup beef or lamb broth
    1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
    1 cup frozen peas
    Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

For the mashed potato topping:
    2 lbs russet potatoes, peeled and quartered
    1/2 cup milk (or half-and-half for extra creaminess)
    4 tablespoons unsalted butter
    Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
    Optional: 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese for topping

Equipment:
    Large pot
    Large skillet or Dutch oven
    Potato masher
    9x13 inch baking dish

Instructions:

1. Get those mashed potatoes going:

    Toss your peeled, chopped potatoes into a big pot. Cover with cold water and throw in a teaspoon of salt.
    Bring to a boil and cook until the potatoes are fork-tender, about 15-20 minutes.
    Drain 'em well, then pop them back in the pot.   
    Add the milk and butter, then mash to your heart’s content until they’re smooth and dreamy. Season generously with salt and pepper to taste. Set 'em aside for now.

2. Cook up that savory lamb filling:

    While the potatoes do their thing, heat some olive oil in a big skillet or Dutch oven over medium heat.
    Add the chopped onion, carrots, and celery (if using). Sauté until the vegetables are softened, about 5-7 minutes.
    Stir in the garlic and let it cook for another minute—just enough to wake up the flavor.   
    Add the ground lamb and break it up with a spoon as it cooks. Once it’s nice and browned, drain off any extra fat.
    Sprinkle in the rosemary and thyme, then stir in the flour and let it cook for a minute to thicken things up.
    Add the tomato paste and cook for another minute, stirring.
    Pour in the beef or lamb broth and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan.
    Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 10-15 minutes, or until the sauce has thickened slightly.
    Stir in the peas and let them warm through for a few minutes. Season with salt and pepper until it tastes just right.

3. Time to build your masterpiece:

    Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
    Pour the lamb filling into the bottom of the 9x13 inch baking dish, spreading it out evenly.
    Spoon the mashed potatoes on top—start at the edges to seal it in, then spread it all over.
    Want to take it up a notch? Sprinkle on some shredded cheddar cheese. Totally optional, but highly recommended.
    Use a fork to fluff up the top—those little ridges get nice and crispy in the oven.
    Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
    Let the Shepherd's Pie rest for 10-15 minutes before serving. This allows it to set slightly and makes it easier to serve.

Serve it up, grab a cozy spot, and enjoy your warm, comforting Shepherd’s Pie!
